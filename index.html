Section 1 • Background & Context

The Strategic Imperative & The Four Gaps

Technological disruption is fundamentally altering how citizens experience justice. Grounding digital transformation in Gross National Happiness and constitutional values requires closing four interconnected structural gaps.

Global Poly-Crises & Disruptive AI

The Global Challenge

Justice systems worldwide face unprecedented headwinds: climate breakdown, extreme inequality, authoritarian algorithmic controls, and unguided AI disruption causing institutional anxiety, burnout, and privacy erosion.

Opaque black-box AI systems with embedded socio-cultural biases.
Surveillance creep and unvetted algorithmic decision-making.
Commercial tech deployments divorced from human dignity.

The Bhutanese GNH Opportunity

Constitutional Antidote

Bhutan is uniquely positioned to model mindful technology governance: shaping digital evolution through compassion, human agency, constitutional harmony, and holistic wellbeing.

Human dignity and mindfulness placed firmly at the centre of code.
Gross National Happiness (GNH) algorithmic impact auditing.
Inclusive, disability-friendly, Dzongkha-native public justice.

Knowledge Gap • Academic & Legal Implication Research

Prior to JusTICe, Bhutan lacked an institutional anchor studying the intersection of emerging AI, multi-agent workflows, data privacy, and judicial independence. JusTICe provides horizon scanning, academic scholarship, and GNH-aligned policy inputs.

Mandate I Core Focus

Empirical Diagnostics: July 2026 Multi-Agency Consultations

Representatives from 17 key Bhutanese institutions convened at JSW Law School to identify urgent systemic pain points in the justice ecosystem:

Bottleneck 01

Paper-Based Delays

Heavy reliance on physical case files causing administrative delays and extended case resolution periods.

Bottleneck 02

Isolated Data Silos

Fragmented repositories across police, prosecutors, and courts creating uncoordinated case histories.

Bottleneck 03

Rugged Geography

Mountainous terrain and long distances isolating rural gewogs from physical judicial infrastructure.

Bottleneck 04

Linguistic & UX Gaps

Need for Dzongkha-native legal AI, voice-enabled interfaces, and universal accessibility for illiterate citizens.
